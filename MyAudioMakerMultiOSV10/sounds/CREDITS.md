# Sound library credits

MyAudioMaker plays real recorded instruments from these free sample libraries. Each was cut to a subset of notes,
trimmed, level-normalized, looped where notes sustain, resampled to 48 kHz and encoded to Ogg Opus by `scripts/fetch-sounds.mjs`.
The full licence texts are in `licenses/`.

- Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL (commit `c1ea7bcc3c`)
