opus-decoder 0.7.9 and @wasm-audio-decoders/common 9.0.6 (https://github.com/eshaz/wasm-audio-decoders)
Decode the bundled sound library's Ogg Opus samples (src/audio/ogg-opus.js). The packages declare the MIT
licence and ship no licence file; this is the MIT licence text with the author named in the packages.
They contain libopus compiled to WebAssembly: see libopus.LICENSE.txt (BSD 3-Clause, Xiph.Org and others).
The bundled sounds themselves are credited in sounds/CREDITS.md and sounds/licenses/.
