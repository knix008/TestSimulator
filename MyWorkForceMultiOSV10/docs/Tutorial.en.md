# MyWorkForce Tutorial

This document walks through MyWorkForce's main features with the **Online shop** example, from start to finish. Following the chapters in order takes about 30 minutes. **Start the tutorial** inside the program shows the same steps as hints in a corner of the screen.

> Opening an example replaces the current workspace. With a database that holds real work, practise on a test database or the web demo.

## 1. Getting in

1. Start MyWorkForce; the start page opens.
2. Click **Sign in** at the top and enter your account and password. The first time, use the administrator account `admin` and choose a new password.
3. **Close** shuts the sign-in window at any time; the guides, background and language on the start page need no sign-in.
4. Use **Start page settings** in the top row to choose the cards, and **Auto arrange** to lay them out neatly.

## 2. Opening an example

1. On the **Examples** card click **Software → Online shop**.
2. Click **Continue** when asked, and the shop workspace opens.
3. The `SHOP` project appears under **My work → My projects** on the left.

> There are 30 examples: ten software, ten business and ten service. Open one close to your own work.

## 3. Looking around a project

1. Open **This project → Board** on the left. The active sprint's cards sit in their columns.
2. Drag a card from **To do** to **In progress** to change its status.
3. **Double-click** a card to open the issue, and change its assignee and priority in the right panel.
4. Open **Backlog** and drag issues into the sprint.

## 4. Creating and changing issues

1. Click **+** on the toolbar (Ctrl+N), type `Coupon is not applied` as the summary, and create it.
2. On the **Comments** tab, call a colleague with `@name` and post with Ctrl+Enter.
3. Set **Start** and **Due** in the right panel.
4. On the **Links** tab choose **blocks** and type another issue's key. The blocked issue shows **Waiting for** in red.

## 5. Planning a sprint

1. On **Backlog**, click **Sprint** to make a new sprint.
2. Drag issues into it and click **Start**.
3. When the work is done, close it with **Complete sprint**; unfinished issues go back to the backlog or the next sprint.
4. Check the burndown and velocity under **Reports**.

## 6. The timeline and dependencies

1. Open **Timeline** on the left. Issues are bars under their epics.
2. Drag a bar to move its dates, and drag an end to make it longer.
3. Drag the dot on a bar's right onto another bar to make a "blocks" link; an arrow appears.
4. Make the earlier issue end later and the arrow turns red, with a warning at the top.

## 7. Workspaces and pages

1. Under **Company → Workspaces** on the left, click **New workspace**, type `Shop docs` in the box and press Enter. Set its description and who sees it under **Workspace properties** in the right panel.
2. Click **+** on its row to open the **Templates** window, and double-click **Meetings → Meeting notes**.
3. The page opens ready to write. Fill in the date and attendees; it saves by itself.
4. On the formatting bar choose **Insert issues → Query list** with `SHOP` and `status != done`. **Preview** shows the open issues as a table.
5. Use **+** on the page's row to add child pages, like chapters and sections.

## 8. Writing together and commenting

1. Select a sentence and use the **Comment** button that appears to comment on that passage; it turns yellow.
2. Continue with **Reply**, and click **Resolve** when the discussion is over.
3. Drop a file on the page's **Files** area to attach it.
4. When someone else opens the same page, "Writing with …" appears and each person's writing shows up on the other's screen.
5. Under **Earlier versions** in the right panel, click **Compare** to see what changed.

## 9. Finding things and getting notified

1. Click the **magnifier** on the toolbar (Ctrl+Shift+F) and search for `payment`. Projects, issues, pages, comments and files come back by kind.
2. Click a result to go there.
3. When a colleague calls you with `@name`, the bell on the toolbar gets a number; click the notice under **Notifications** to go to the page.
4. Open MyWorkForce Talk with **Talk** to see your notices room and chats.

## 10. Next steps

- Create real **teams** and **projects** and give people access.
- In a project's **Settings**, set its methods, workflow and automation rules.
- The **User's guide** covers everything in detail; the **WQL guide** has the query grammar.
- **Start the tutorial** on the start page replays the on-screen hints.
