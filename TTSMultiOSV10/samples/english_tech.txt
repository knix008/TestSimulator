Text-to-speech technology has advanced significantly in recent years.
Modern TTS systems use deep learning models trained on thousands of hours of speech data.
These models can produce natural-sounding voices with proper intonation and rhythm.
Applications range from accessibility tools for visually impaired users to voice assistants.
The Kokoro 82M model used in this application achieves high quality English synthesis.
Future developments will bring even more realistic and expressive speech generation.
