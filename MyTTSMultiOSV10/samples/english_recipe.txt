Here is a simple recipe for fluffy pancakes.
First, whisk one cup of flour with two teaspoons of baking powder and a pinch of salt.
In another bowl, mix one egg, one cup of milk and two tablespoons of melted butter.
Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir until just combined.
Cook small circles of batter in a hot pan for about two minutes on each side.
Serve them warm with fresh fruit and a little maple syrup.
