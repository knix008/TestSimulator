# Viewport layout · synchronised scrolling · reference lines

Place several images side by side to compare them.

![Viewport layout](images/help-layout.jpg)

## Viewport layout

Choose 1×1, 1×2, 2×1, 2×2, 1×3, 2×3 or 3×3 from **View › Viewport layout** or the layout button on the toolbar.

- Click a viewport to make it the **active viewport**; tools, window settings and exports apply to the active viewport.
- Images from your other open tabs fill the empty viewports in order. Files you open later appear in the next viewport.

## Synchronised scrolling

Turn on **View › Synchronised scrolling** and, when you scroll slices in one viewport, the others follow to the slice at the **same position in patient coordinates**. Use it to compare a previous study with today's, or CT with MR.

## Reference lines

Turn on **View › Reference lines** to see, as a line, where the active viewport's slice cuts the images in the other viewports (for example the coronal and sagittal positions on an axial view).

> Synchronised scrolling and reference lines need position information in the images (Image Position · Orientation). Set the defaults in Settings › Viewer.
