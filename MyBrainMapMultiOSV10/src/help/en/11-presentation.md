# Presentation

![Presentation](images/presentation.png)

*A presentation: the current branch stays sharp, the rest fades.*

`F5` (View › Presentation) presents the map full screen.

- The first slide is the whole map, then **one slide per main topic**, then the floating topics, and the whole map again at the end.
- On each slide the view glides to that branch and the other topics fade.
- `→` `Space` `PageDown` `Enter` or a click: next · `←` `PageUp`: previous · `Home` / `End`: first / last · `Esc`: end.
- The bar at the bottom shows the slide's title and number.

The slides follow the order of the main topics; reorder them on the map with `Alt+↑` / `Alt+↓` or by dragging. Folded branches stay folded.
