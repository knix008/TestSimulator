MyMarkdownMaster — Tutorial

The same steps the tutorial shows in the program (Help › Tutorial, Shift+F1).

Start

1. MyMarkdownMaster

A folder is a vault. Join its Markdown notes with [[links]], explore them with the graph, canvases and search, and write with split editing and preview.

2. Actions

Open a folder as a vault or make a new one. On the web, a vault can also live inside the browser.

3. Sample vaults

Open the guide, Zettelkasten, project, journal, research or novel sample as a fresh vault.

4. Background

Pick the moving background and card opacity. Cards can be dragged and resized.

Vault & views

5. Files

The file tree. Fold folders, drag files onto folders to move them or onto a pane to open them there. The panel folds away with ‹.

6. Live view

Write with the formatting shown; only the line with the cursor shows its Markdown marks.

7. Links

Type [[ to complete note names; #heading, ^block and |alias work too. ![[ ]] embeds another note.

8. Reading view

The finished note: math, diagrams, charts, callouts and task checkboxes all work.

9. Split view

Source on the left, preview on the right, scrolling together. Drag the divider, and fold either side with ‹ ›.

10. Panes

Split panes right or down, drag tabs into other panes or onto an edge. Save the arrangement as a workspace.

Find & connect

11. Search

The Rust core searches the whole vault: tag:, path:, file:, line:( ), section:( ), task-todo:, [property:value], /regex/.

12. Backlinks

Notes that link here, and mentions without a link — one click turns them into links.

13. Outline

The headings. Click to jump; fold sub-headings away.

14. Graph

A map of notes and links. Drag, zoom, filter, colour groups and tune the forces. The right panel has the local graph of the current note.

15. Command palette

Ctrl+P finds any command, Ctrl+O finds a note by name.

Write

16. Properties

The note's YAML properties: edit them in the table — tags, aliases, dates.

17. Today's note

Alt+D opens today's note. Its template and folder are in Settings › Notes.

18. Writing tools

Focus mode, typewriter scrolling, style check, slides. The Statistics tab shows the word goal and reading time.

19. Canvas

An endless board of cards and notes joined by lines (JSON Canvas).

20. AI assistant

Connect OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Ollama and more: chat, and summarize, improve, translate or tag your notes.

Export & settings

21. Export

PDF, Word, ODT, EPUB, RTF, LaTeX, HTML and more — 18 formats. Export again with Ctrl+Alt+E.

22. Status bar

Backlinks, words and characters, reading time, goal progress, line and column, save state, problems.

23. Themes

Many light and dark themes, plus reading themes (creamy, solarized, high contrast …).

24. Settings

Editor, reading, files & links, notes, writing, export and AI, one tab each.

25. Done

Replay the tutorial any time. You are going back to your own vault now.
